How Hilltoppers Study works

Sign in with your school email and the same password as the Hilltoppers extension. Tick the blocks you have study hall; your name then shows to classmates who want to study.

Chat and screen sharing go straight between you and your classmate. The server only matches you up and never sees your messages or your screen.

Screen sharing needs permission in the Hilltoppers popup. If the Share button does nothing, ask to have it enabled.
